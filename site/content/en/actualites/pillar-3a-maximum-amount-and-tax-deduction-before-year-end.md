Contributing to pillar 3a before December 31 allows a tax deduction for the year. Maximum amounts are set by the federal office: 7,258 francs for those with a pension fund, and up to 36,288 francs for self-employed or those without a pension fund. Only contributions made within the year are considered.

## What are the maximum allowed amounts in 2026?

In 2026, the maximum deductible amount for people with a pension fund is 7,258 francs. Those without a pension fund or who are self-employed can contribute up to 20% of their income, capped at 36,288 francs per year. These limits are published by the federal office and cantonal tax authorities.

Amounts are adjusted periodically: they were 7,056 francs for employees and 35,280 francs for self-employed in 2024. For 2027, the limits will be 7,373 francs and 36,864 francs respectively. Official deduction tables are available on cantonal websites.

Any excess over the maximum is not allowed and must be refunded by the pension institution. Calculation tools help verify the applicable maximum based on status and income. Contributions must reach the pension institution before year-end to be counted for the current year.

## How does the tax deduction apply?

The tax deduction applies only to contributions made during the year. Pillar 3a contributions are deductible from taxable income for federal, cantonal and municipal taxes. Employees and those without a pension fund or who are self-employed can reduce their tax burden.

The tax return must state the actual amount contributed, within the annual limit. Cantonal administrations publish detailed instructions for pillar 3a deductions. Simulation tools estimate tax savings by municipality.

Contributions must be made before December 31 to count for the fiscal year. Pension institutions send contribution statements to the tax authorities. Amounts not deductible are refunded by the pension institution.

## What are the conditions for contributing to pillar 3a?

To contribute to pillar 3a, income must be subject to AHV. Employees, those without a pension fund or who are self-employed, and recipients of unemployment benefits can open a pillar 3a account.

People living abroad but working in Switzerland and subject to Swiss social insurance are also eligible. Conditions for opening and contributing are specified by the federal office and pension institutions.

The right to contribute must exist for each year concerned by a contribution or buyback. Tools help check eligibility and the maximum amount based on professional status. Beneficiaries of pillar 3a are defined by law according to succession order.

## What is new about retroactive pillar 3a buybacks?

Since 2026, retroactive buybacks in pillar 3a are possible to fill a contribution gap for 2025. Buybacks are allowed for up to ten years, only for years when the right to contribute existed.

The maximum buyback amount matches the "small contribution": 7,258 francs for 2026. To make a buyback, the right to contribute must have existed for the relevant year and the full ordinary contribution must be paid for the buyback year.

Buybacks are fully deductible from taxable income, just like the annual contribution. Specific rules ensure legality and tracking by tax authorities. The first retroactive buybacks are possible in 2026 for missed 2025 contributions.

## What are pillar 3a account interest rates in 2026?

Interest rates for pillar 3a accounts vary by institution. In 2026, the minimum rate is 0.05%, the median is 0.20%, and the maximum is 0.65%. Annual interest on a 7,258 franc contribution ranges from 3.63 to 47.18 francs depending on the rate.

Rates are taken from official institution pages and may change at any time. Tools let you view historical rates and compare available offers. Remuneration and management conditions are published by each institution.

The part invested in pension funds does not earn the account rate. Base rates apply only to the account balance, with no tier. Management fees and withdrawal terms are listed in official conditions.

## How much tax can be saved with a pillar 3a contribution?

Tax savings from pillar 3a depend on the amount contributed, taxable income and municipality. For a 7,258 franc contribution in 2026, a single person with 80,000 francs taxable income saves between 1,640.35 and 2,357.55 francs depending on the municipality.

Savings vary: in Geneva, from 1,857.60 to 2,112.65 francs; in Vaud, from 1,848.60 to 2,139.40 francs; in Valais, from 1,982.60 to 2,357.55 francs. Tools let you simulate tax savings for each municipality.

The tax return must state the actual amount contributed and respect annual limits. Pension institutions send contribution statements to the tax authorities. Tax savings are calculated according to official scales.
