In every premium region of French-speaking Switzerland, the highest basic health insurance offer in 2027 costs 29% to 50% more than the lowest for the same adult with a CHF 300 deductible and accident cover. Over a year, the difference ranges from CHF 1,743.60 to CHF 3,648. The figures come from the 2027 premiums that the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) approved and published on Priminfo.

## How large is the gap in each premium region?

Geneva has the widest gap, with the highest offer 50% above the lowest, and region VS 1 (Sion) the narrowest, at 29%. The table gives the lowest, median and highest monthly premium in each region for an adult with a CHF 300 deductible and accident cover, across all insurance models.

| Neuchâtel | CHF 583.40 | CHF 648.80 | CHF 835.00 | 43% | CHF 3,019.20 |
| Jura | CHF 584.30 | CHF 642.30 | CHF 810.60 | 39% | CHF 2,715.60 |

For this profile, each region has more than a hundred offers, from 105 in Jura to 129 in region BE 2. An offer is one insurer's product in one model. The FOPH sets the premium regions. Geneva, Neuchâtel and Jura each form a single region, while Vaud, Fribourg and Valais have two each.

## Where does such a gap come from?

It adds up two kinds of difference, between insurers and between insurance models. Alternative models (family doctor, care network, telemedicine) restrict the choice of provider, which the law allows in return for a lower premium (art. 41 para. 4 and art. 62 LAMal).

If you keep only the standard model, with free choice of doctor, the gap for the same adult narrows to between 14% in region VS 1 and 29% in Geneva. In Geneva, the lowest standard premium is then CHF 711.20 a month and the highest CHF 915.70. A good share of the overall gap therefore comes from the choice of model, and the rest from the insurer.

The law sets the list of care that basic insurance covers (art. 34 LAMal), so the benefits are the same whichever insurer you pick. A higher premium does not buy wider cover. Customer service, reimbursement times and the rules of an alternative model can still differ from one insurer to another.

## What does the median show?

The median splits a region's offers into two halves, one cheaper and one more expensive. In Geneva it is CHF 686.05 a month for an adult with a CHF 300 deductible and accident cover, CHF 74.35 above the lowest offer. In region VS 2 (Brig-Glis) it is CHF 498.35.

The median ignores how many people each insurer covers. Our statistics give every offer the same weight and describe the range of prices on offer. The FOPH publishes the average premium actually paid in each canton, and our premium barometer shows it.

## How can you see the offers in your own region?

Your premium region depends on your municipality. With your postcode, year of birth and deductible, Priminfo or our premium tool lists the approved 2027 premiums for your profile from lowest to highest. No insurer pays to appear on our list or for its position on it.

To switch insurer on 1 January 2027, your basic insurance cancellation has to reach your current insurer by the last working day of November (art. 7 para. 2 LAMal). Our letter tool works out that date and drafts the letter.

## How were these figures calculated?

We took from Priminfo the approved 2027 premium of every offer for an adult born in 1990, with a CHF 300 deductible and accident cover, in each premium region of French-speaking Switzerland. The minimum, median and maximum cover every offer in the region. The percentage gap compares the highest offer with the lowest, and the yearly gap is the monthly difference multiplied by twelve. The Bernese Jura belongs to premium region BE 2, which also includes German-speaking municipalities.
