Average adult health insurance premiums go up in all six French-speaking cantons in 2027, by 3.2% in Vaud at the low end and 5.9% in Jura at the high end. For Switzerland as a whole, the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) puts the increase at 4.9%, from CHF 464.80 to CHF 487.60 a month. The FOPH published these figures on 29 September 2026, and they apply to the whole of 2027.

## How much do premiums rise in each canton?

Jura, Valais, Neuchâtel and Fribourg rise faster than the Swiss average, while Geneva and Vaud rise more slowly. The table shows the FOPH's average premiums for an adult aged 26 or over, exactly as published.

| Canton | 2026 average | 2027 average | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jura | CHF 520.50 | CHF 551.00 | 5.9% |
| Valais | CHF 436.10 | CHF 460.90 | 5.7% |
| Neuchâtel | CHF 530.80 | CHF 558.80 | 5.3% |
| Fribourg | CHF 442.60 | CHF 465.50 | 5.2% |
| Geneva | CHF 585.40 | CHF 610.60 | 4.3% |
| Vaud | CHF 526.60 | CHF 543.50 | 3.2% |
| Switzerland | CHF 464.80 | CHF 487.60 | 4.9% |

Geneva still has the highest average premium in French-speaking Switzerland, at CHF 610.60 a month. Valais has the lowest, at CHF 460.90, even after one of the sharpest increases.

## What does that mean in francs?

The average premium goes up by CHF 16.90 a month in Vaud and by CHF 30.50 in Jura, the two ends of the range. An adult paying exactly the average would therefore pay between CHF 202.80 and CHF 366.00 more over the year.

Ranked in francs, the order changes a little. Neuchâtel, at CHF 28.00 more a month, moves ahead of Valais (CHF 24.80) because its premiums start from a higher level. The monthly increase is CHF 25.20 in Geneva and CHF 22.90 in Fribourg. Across Switzerland, the average adult premium goes up by CHF 22.80 a month.

## What do young adults and children pay on average?

The FOPH also publishes 2027 averages for young adults aged 19 to 25 and for children. A young adult's average premium runs from CHF 310.10 a month in Valais to CHF 453.00 in Geneva, against CHF 338.80 for Switzerland. A child's runs from CHF 116.50 in Valais to CHF 157.70 in Geneva, against CHF 128.10 nationally.

A household with two adults and two children pays four premiums, and the four increases add up. Premium reductions granted by the cantons can ease the bill for households on modest incomes. The conditions differ from canton to canton, and the cantonal office in charge can tell you what they are.

## Why is the average different from my premium?

The FOPH average is a cantonal indicator. It blends optional deductibles, alternative models (family doctor, telemedicine, care networks) and the insurers active in the canton, so it sits below the premium of an adult with the CHF 300 deductible and free choice of doctor.

Your own premium depends on your premium region, age, deductible, model and insurer. A canton can have more than one premium region, and in 2027 Vaud, Fribourg and Valais each have two. Within one region, for an adult with the CHF 300 deductible and accident cover, the highest offer costs between CHF 1,743.60 and CHF 3,648 a year more than the lowest. Our barometer breaks these gaps down by canton and region without naming any insurer.

The FOPH adds that the 2027 averages are projections based on the expected number of insured people and may still move slightly. For 2026, the actual increase turned out to be 4.3%, against the 4.4% announced in 2025.

## What can you do before the end of November?

Your insurer's letter states your 2027 premium. With that figure you can look up the approved premiums for your region on Priminfo, the FOPH's official site, or in our premium tool, which lists offers from lowest to highest.

For basic insurance with the ordinary deductible, your cancellation letter has to reach the insurer by the last working day of November (art. 7 para. 2 LAMal). Posting it by registered mail a few days early leaves room for postal delays. The new insurer, for its part, has to accept anyone living in its area of activity (art. 4 para. 2 LAMal).

Supplementary insurance follows other rules. It falls under the Insurance Contract Act, and the insurer may ask health questions before accepting a new customer. If you cancel a supplementary policy before another insurer has accepted you in writing, you may end up without cover.

## Where do these figures come from?

The 2026 and 2027 averages come from the FOPH table of average monthly cantonal premiums published in September 2026. We use them unchanged, with the FOPH's own rounding to the centime and to a tenth of a percent. The increase in francs is the difference between the two published amounts, which we multiply by twelve to get the yearly figure.
