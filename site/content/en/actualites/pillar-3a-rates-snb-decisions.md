The Swiss National Bank (SNB) policy rate has stood at 0% since 20 June 2025 and has not moved at any assessment since. In late November 2026, pillar 3a savings accounts at the 17 banks we track pay between 0.05% and 0.65% a year, with a median of 0.20%. The SNB holds its next assessment on 10 December 2026.

## What has the SNB decided since 2025?

The SNB cut its policy rate twice in 2025 and has held it since. On 20 March 2025 it lowered the rate from 0.50% to 0.25%, effective the next day. On 19 June 2025 it cut it to 0%, effective 20 June 2025.

It then kept the rate at 0% at each quarterly assessment, on 25 September 2025, 11 December 2025, 19 March 2026, 18 June 2026 and 24 September 2026. Its official schedule puts the next assessment on 10 December 2026, with a press release at 9:30. The 2027 dates are 18 March, 24 June, 23 September and 16 December.

## What does a 3a account pay today?

On 22 November 2026 we recorded the base rate on 3a accounts at 17 banks active in French-speaking Switzerland, using only their official pages and rate sheets. The lowest rate is 0.05% and the highest 0.65%. Eleven of the 17 banks pay 0.20% or less.

The base rate applies to an adult from the first franc. Some banks publish a special rate for young savers or for a service package. Our pillar 3a rate table lists those variants along with each bank's name.

Over one year the difference in francs is small. A CHF 7,373 contribution earning 0.20% brings in about CHF 14.75 of interest in the first year, against about CHF 47.92 at 0.65%. These examples ignore later contributions and any change in rates.

## Have banks changed their rates since the cut?

Seven of the 17 banks show a rate valid from a date in 2026, between 1 February and 1 November, which means they reviewed it after the last policy rate cut. The other ten publish no validity date.

Our weekly survey started on 22 November 2026, and the first two readings, on 22 and 23 November, show no change. We therefore cannot yet put a figure on how far banks adjusted their 3a rates after the 2025 decisions. The history grows every Monday and can be downloaded as a CSV file.

## Does the 3a rate follow the policy rate?

No. Each bank sets the rate on its 3a accounts and can change it at any time. The policy rate steers the general level of Swiss interest rates, yet no rule ties a 3a account rate to an SNB decision, either in size or in timing. If the SNB holds on 10 December, a bank is still free to change its 3a rate, and a cut would not force it to.

## Is pillar 3a still worth it with rates this low?

Pillar 3a keeps its tax advantage whatever the account pays. Contributions are deductible from taxable income up to the maximum amounts (art. 33 para. 1 let. e of the Federal Direct Tax Act and art. 7 BVV 3). In 2027 the cap is CHF 7,373 for people with a pension fund, and 20% of income, up to CHF 36,864, for those without one.

The tax saving depends on your canton, municipality and income. For a maximum contribution, it can far exceed a year's interest. Our calculator estimates it with the 2026 tax scales of the French-speaking cantons. A contribution has to reach the account by 31 December to count for that tax year.

Pillar 3a also comes as securities-based solutions and insurance policies, whose returns, costs and risks differ from those of a savings account. This article covers 3a savings accounts only and does not compare these other products.
