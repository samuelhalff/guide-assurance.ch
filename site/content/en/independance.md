This is a translation. The [French version](/independance/) prevails.

Guide Assurance explains insurance in French-speaking Switzerland and presents public figures. No place in its lists is for sale, and the site recommends no contract.

## Can an insurer pay for a higher place?

No. No insurer can pay to appear, to move up a list or to receive a badge. When a tool displays insurers, the list is complete and sorted by a criterion that is visible on screen (see [Methodology](/en/methodology/)).

## Does the site recommend an insurer?

No. The site presents the rules, the figures and the differences, and the choice of contract is yours. Under art. 182a OS (Insurance Supervision Ordinance), advising a person with a view to concluding a contract, or offering them one, is an insurance intermediation activity. Guide Assurance does not carry out this activity.

Sorting by premium is not advice, because the premium says nothing about service, the requirements of a model or your health situation.

## Who publishes the site?

The publisher of the site and its contact details are given in the [legal notice](/en/legal-notice/).

## Being called back by an adviser

If you ask for it, an adviser from a partner insurance broker in French-speaking Switzerland calls you back. It is the only broker that receives requests from the site. The form says what kind of recipient receives the request before you send it, and the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/#requests) describes what is stored.

## What this page does not cover

This page does not describe how data is processed (see [Privacy](/en/privacy/)) or our sources (see [Methodology](/en/methodology/)). The legal definition of insurance intermediation is in [art. 182a OS on Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2005/735/fr#art_182_a).
