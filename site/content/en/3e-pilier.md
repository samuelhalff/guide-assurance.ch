In 2027 you can pay up to CHF 7,373 into a pillar 3a if you belong to a pension fund, or 20% of your income up to CHF 36,864 if you do not (Federal Council, press release of 02.10.2026).

Pillar 3 is optional private pension provision, on top of the AVS (old-age and survivors’ insurance, 1st pillar) and the pension fund (LPP, occupational pensions, 2nd pillar). Pillar 3a is tied pension savings, tax-deductible within these limits. Pillar 3b is flexible savings whose tax treatment depends on the cantons.

## How much can you pay into a 3a in 2027?

The Federal Council published the 2027 limits on 02.10.2026:

| Situation | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Member of a pension fund (LPP) | CHF 7,258 | **CHF 7,373** |
| No pension fund | 20% of income, max. CHF 36,288 | **20% of income, max. CHF 36,864** |

These amounts are 8% and 40% of the upper LPP limit amount (art. 7 para. 1 let. a and b OPP 3, the ordinance on tied pension savings). The State of Geneva publishes the same figures.

The limit applies per person and per year, so each working spouse has their own (art. 7 para. 2 OPP 3). Payments remain possible up to 5 years at most after the reference age (art. 7 para. 3 OPP 3).

## Can you make up missed 3a years?

Yes, for the previous 10 years, provided you pay the full contribution for the year of the buy-back. The buy-back comes on top of the ordinary contribution and is capped each year at 8% of the upper LPP limit amount (art. 7a OPP 3). For 2027, this cap is CHF 7,373, which is 8% of the upper LPP limit amount of CHF 92,160 published by the FSIO. The rule has applied since 01.01.2025 (RO 2024 622), so only gaps that arose from 2025 onwards can be bought back.

## What is the difference between 3a and 3b?

Pillar 3a is tied savings, held in a bank account or an insurance policy. Payments are deductible within the limits above, and the money can be withdrawn only in the cases listed in art. 3 OPP 3. Pillar 3b is flexible savings, and its tax treatment depends on your canton.

## How much does a 3a account pay, and how much tax do you save?

In our survey of 22.11.2026, bank 3a accounts paid between 0.05% and 0.65% a year. The [pillar 3a interest rate table](/en/pillar-3a/interest-rates/) lists them from the highest rate to the lowest. The tax saving depends on your income, municipality and family situation; the [pillar 3a tax savings calculator](/en/pillar-3a/tax-savings/) estimates it with the 2026 tax scales for Geneva, Vaud, Valais, Fribourg, Neuchâtel, Jura and the Bernese Jura. The [3a rate history](/en/pillar-3a/interest-rates/history/) shows how these rates move from one survey to the next, and the [tax saving by municipality](/en/pillar-3a/tax-savings/municipalities/) page compares municipalities for the same payment.

## Can pillar 3 cover death and disability?

Yes. A life insurance policy can cover pure risk (a capital paid out on death or disability) or combine risk and savings. Some insurance-based 3a contracts provide a premium waiver in case of disability, which is worth checking in each offer.

## How do 3a products differ?

The first choice is between a bank 3a and an insurance 3a, which may buy pure risk, savings or both. Products then differ in fees, returns and the share of the return the provider commits to pay under the contract. For an insurance policy, the premium waiver in case of disability, the death capital, the commitment period and the surrender value also matter.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. An insurance 3a policy runs for many years, and an early surrender can lose you money (surrender value).
2. There is no couple limit: each working spouse has their own (art. 7 para. 2 OPP 3).
3. The “no LPP” limit applies only to people who are not members of a pension fund. It does not apply automatically to everyone who is self-employed.
4. A life insurance policy cannot be cancelled like other contracts, because it is excluded from the ordinary right of cancellation in art. 35a LCA, the Insurance Contract Act (art. 35a para. 3 LCA).

## How and when can you cancel?

The right of withdrawal lasts 14 days from the proposal or the acceptance of the contract (art. 2a LCA). Life insurance is not subject to ordinary cancellation under art. 35a LCA; the surrender conditions of the contract apply instead (art. 90 ff. LCA). If something was not disclosed when the contract was concluded, art. 6 LCA applies. A bank 3a account is closed according to the foundation’s regulations, and withdrawal remains limited to the cases in art. 3 OPP 3.
