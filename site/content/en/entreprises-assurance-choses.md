Property insurance protects a company’s assets: furniture and fittings, machinery, electronics and goods. Business interruption insurance, often taken out with it, covers the loss of turnover that follows a loss event. No federal law requires them. Vaud, Fribourg and Jura do require businesses to insure their contents against fire: with the ECA (cantonal building insurance institution) in Vaud, with a private insurer in Fribourg and Jura.

## Is property insurance compulsory?

Federal law does not require it. Three French-speaking cantons, however, require businesses to insure their contents.

- In Vaud, business contents must be insured with the ECA against fire and natural hazards (State of Vaud).
- In Fribourg, they must be insured against fire and forces of nature with a licensed private insurer (law RSF 732.2.1, art. 1 and 4). The employer also insures its employees’ personal belongings on its premises (art. 3).
- In Jura, they must be insured against fire with a private insurer, including valuables used in the activity (law RSJU 873.21, art. 2 and 7).

For Neuchâtel, Geneva and Valais, we found no obligation for business contents in the cantonal law. No regulatory figure applies in 2027. Buildings have their own page: [Building insurance](/en/building-insurance/).

## What can you compare?

The first difference is inventory and goods, meaning the sum insured and how it is valued. Contracts then vary on theft, water damage, machinery breakdown and electronics (computers, servers, tills), and on the value used, new replacement value or lower. For business interruption, the question is which turnover loss is indemnified and for how long. Each contract also sets its own underinsurance rules.

## What are the common pitfalls?

They concern the sum insured, business interruption and, in Vaud, overlap with the ECA.

1. Stock is underinsured in the high season. If the sum insured is based on average stock, the indemnity may be reduced after a loss at the peak of the season.
2. The business has no business interruption insurance. After a fire or water damage, it is often what allows the business to hold on during the repairs.
3. In Vaud, the same risk is paid for twice. Contents are already insured with the ECA against fire, so it is worth checking that the private contract does not cover them a second time.

## What should you prepare before asking for a quote?

An up-to-date inventory and the value of your stock, including the high season, are the basis of the request. A list of the machinery and electronics to cover helps, along with an estimate of how long the business could be stopped after a loss. These figures set the sums insured and the indemnity period, which you can then compare from one quote to the next.

## How do you terminate a property policy?

Ordinary termination takes effect at the end of the 3rd year, then at the end of each year, with 3 months’ notice (art. 35a LCA). After a claim, art. 42 LCA applies. In Vaud, the ECA contract cannot be terminated like a private contract.

IT risks (cyberattack, ransomware) fall under another contract: see [Business cyber insurance](/en/business/cyber-insurance/). Damage caused to third parties falls under [business liability insurance](/en/business/professional-liability/).
