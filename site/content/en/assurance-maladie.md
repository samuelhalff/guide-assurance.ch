Basic health insurance (LAMal) is compulsory for everyone living in Switzerland. Its benefits are set by law and do not vary from one insurer to another. In 2027 the average premium is CHF 412.00 a month (FOPH, published 29.09.2026); yours depends on the insurer, the deductible you choose and the insurance model.

## Who must be insured, and by when?

Anyone who takes up residence in Switzerland, or is born there, must take out insurance within 3 months (art. 3 para. 1 LAMal). Insurers are required to accept anyone who applies to join basic insurance (FOPH press release of 29.09.2026).

For people who have just arrived, the deadline and retroactive cover are explained on the [Moving to Switzerland](/en/moving-to-switzerland/) page. If you live in France and work in Switzerland, the [Cross-border workers](/en/cross-border-workers/) page covers your situation.

## How much does basic insurance cost in 2027?

The average 2027 premium, all insured persons combined, is CHF 412.00 a month, up 5.0% (FOPH, premiums published on 29.09.2026). On the Swiss average, an adult pays CHF 487.60 a month, a young adult CHF 338.80 and a child CHF 128.10.

Averages by French-speaking canton and the gap between insurers are on the [2027 premiums](/en/health-insurance/premiums/) page.

## What do you pay on top of the premium?

On top of the premium you pay the deductible, then a co-payment on costs above it. The deductible is the share of costs you bear alone each year. For an adult it is at least CHF 300 (art. 103 para. 1 OAMal) and can be raised to CHF 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000 or 2,500 (art. 93 para. 1 OAMal). Children have no basic deductible (art. 64 para. 4 LAMal), although an optional deductible of CHF 100 to 600, in steps of 100, is possible.

The co-payment is 10% of costs above the deductible (art. 64 para. 2 let. b LAMal), capped each year at CHF 700 for an adult and CHF 350 for a child (art. 103 para. 2 OAMal). Several children of the same family insured with the same insurer together pay at most the amount for one adult.

During pregnancy, no cost sharing is due from the 13th week until 8 weeks after the birth (art. 64 para. 7 LAMal).

A higher deductible lowers the premium, but leaves more of the costs to you if you fall ill.

## What are the new alternative models in 2027?

An alternative model limits your choice of first point of contact in exchange for a lower premium (art. 62 para. 1 LAMal). From 2027 the FOPH groups them into four categories:

- PRAXIS groups the former “family doctor” and HMO models.
- TEL_DIG replaces the former “telemedicine / other” category.
- PHARM, a new category, makes a partner pharmacy your first point of contact.
- FLEX, also new, lets you choose between several first points of contact.

Even combined with a high deductible, the total reduction cannot exceed 50% of the premium of the ordinary model with a CHF 300 deductible and accident cover (priminfo, FOPH FAQ).

## What can you compare between offers?

- The monthly premium, which depends on your premium region, your age, the deductible and the model.
- The requirements of the model, in particular who to call first and what happens if you do not follow the procedure.
- Accident cover, which can be suspended if the LAA (the accident insurance law) already covers you (see [Accident insurance](/en/accident-insurance/)).
- The maximum annual cost, which is 12 premiums plus the deductible and the maximum co-payment.
- The insurer’s service, since basic benefits are the same everywhere.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. Only the date the insurer receives your letter counts; the postmark does not (priminfo, FAQ).
2. With an alternative model or an optional deductible, you cannot change insurer on 1 July 2027.
3. Your previous affiliation ends only once the new insurer has confirmed cover, so there is no gap in cover (art. 7 para. 5 LAMal).
4. Unpaid premiums, cost sharing or fees block a change of insurer until they are settled (priminfo, FAQ).
5. Private insurance cannot cover the deductible or the co-payment (art. 64 para. 8 LAMal).

## What are the cancellation deadlines?

For a change on 1 January 2027, the notice period is one month after the new premium is announced (art. 7 para. 2 LAMal), so your letter must be received by **Monday 30 November 2026** at the latest.

A change on 1 July 2027 requires 3 months’ notice, meaning a letter received by 31 March 2027 at the latest (art. 7 para. 1 LAMal). It is possible only with the CHF 300 deductible and free choice of doctor (art. 94 para. 2 OAMal).

The deductible can be raised only for the start of a calendar year (art. 94 para. 1 OAMal). A reduction takes effect at the end of a year, with the deadlines of art. 7 LAMal (art. 94 para. 2 OAMal).

Changing basic insurer does not oblige you to cancel your supplementary insurance (art. 7 para. 7 and 8 LAMal); its own deadlines are on the [Supplementary health insurance](/en/supplementary-health-insurance/) page.
