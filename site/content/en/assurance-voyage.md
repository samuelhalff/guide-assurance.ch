Abroad, basic health insurance (LAMal) reimburses emergency care up to twice what the same treatment would have cost in Switzerland (art. 36 OAMal, Health Insurance Ordinance). Travel insurance pays for what goes beyond that cap, for repatriation and, depending on the contract, for trip cancellation.

## What does basic insurance already pay?

LAMal (compulsory basic health insurance) reimburses emergency treatment abroad at up to twice the amount that would have been paid for the same treatment in Switzerland (art. 36 para. 2 and 4 OAMal, the Health Insurance Ordinance). In countries where care is expensive, outside the EU and EFTA, in the United States for example, you may have to pay the difference yourself.

## Is travel insurance compulsory?

It is not compulsory for a person insured in Switzerland. Some Schengen visas require nationals of third countries to hold travel insurance; this page does not cover that European rule.

## What do your existing contracts already cover?

Before taking out travel insurance, it is worth checking what your current contracts provide:

- basic insurance pays for emergencies abroad, at up to twice the Swiss tariff (art. 36 OAMal);
- some supplementary health policies include cover abroad;
- a credit card may include travel or cancellation cover, often on condition that the trip was paid with that card;
- membership of a motoring club sometimes includes travel cover.

There is no public data on travel insurance premiums.

## How do two contracts differ?

Contracts differ first in the ceiling for medical costs abroad beyond what LAMal pays, and in whether they pay for repatriation to Switzerland. For cancellation, what matters is the list of covered reasons and the ceiling per trip. The maximum length of each trip and the area covered (Europe or worldwide) also vary. Part of the cover may overlap with a credit card or a motoring club.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. Pre-existing conditions are often excluded from cancellation cover.
2. Cover linked to a credit card may require that the trip was paid with that card.
3. The cancellation ceiling may be lower than the price of the trip.
4. Your supplementary health insurance may already provide cover abroad, which is worth checking before you sign a new contract (see [Supplementary health insurance](/en/supplementary-health-insurance/)).

## How and when can you cancel?

An annual contract can be cancelled for the end of the 3rd year, then for the end of each year, with 3 months’ notice (art. 35a LCA, the Insurance Contract Act). The right of withdrawal lasts 14 days (art. 2a LCA), except for cover of less than one month, which has none (art. 2a para. 4 LCA).

## What this page does not cover

This page does not compare any premium and does not deal with visa rules. It does not replace the general conditions of your contract, which list the covered cancellation reasons. For care abroad, the reference text is [art. 36 OAMal on Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1995/3867_3867_3867/fr#art_36).
