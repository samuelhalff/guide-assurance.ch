This policy explains which data is processed when you visit guide-assurance.ch, and what your rights are under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nLPD, nFADP).

## Controller

West Bay SA, Genthod (GE), Switzerland, UID number (IDE) CHE-107.892.984.

Contact for any question about your data: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}

## The tools calculate in your browser

The cancellation letter and the cross-border calculator do their calculations in your browser, on your device. The information you enter (name, address, income, household composition) is not sent to our servers. We do not receive it and we do not keep it. It disappears when you close the page, unless you print or save the letter yourself.

## Server logs

The server that hosts guide-assurance.ch records technical access logs: the date, the page requested, the response code and the referring page. They contain neither your IP address nor the identification of your browser. They are used for security and for the proper functioning of the site, and are deleted after 30 days.

## The assistant

The assistant answers your questions from the site’s guides, without personal advice.

- The messages of the current conversation go through our server to an AI service hosted in Europe, which writes the answer. Your IP address is used only to limit the number of messages and is not stored.
- Guide Assurance does not record conversations and does not link them to any call-back request. The AI provider may keep them for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes them. It does not use them to train its models.
- The conversation stays in the open tab, so you find it again when you change page. It disappears when you close the tab or click “New conversation”.
- Do not write health information or contact details in the chat. The assistant does not ask for them. If you want a call back, it opens the call-back form, where you check your answers and give your consent.
- With the first message, a bot check confirms the request comes from a person.
- Only opening the assistant, the number of messages and moving on to the form are counted, without the content of the messages.

<a id="requests"></a>

## Call-back requests

If you fill in the form to be called back, West Bay SA stores what you enter (need, postcode, year of birth, household size, name, phone and, if you give them, e-mail and call-back slot) together with proof of your consent: the accepted text, its version, the date and the IP address of the submission.

- The request is passed only to a partner insurance broker in French-speaking Switzerland, which calls you back and becomes the controller from then on. Its name is given to you on request. It is passed to no other company.
- The supplementary health covers you are interested in (hospital, dental) are only processed with your separate express consent, and stored encrypted.
- No analytics tool receives what you type in the form. Analytics tools are not loaded on its pages.
- Requests are hosted in Switzerland. Internal notifications go through a business e-mail service. A bot check confirms the request comes from a person. The list of processors is available on request.
- A request with no follow-up is anonymised after 18 months. Proof of consent is kept separately for 5 years. Encrypted backups are deleted after 30 days.
- You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## What we do not do with your data

We do not pass on any data to an insurer. The only transfer to an intermediary is the one you request yourself through the call-back form, described above.

## Your rights

Under the nLPD, you can request:

- access to the data concerning you;
- rectification if it is inaccurate;
- erasure.

Write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We may ask you to prove your identity before replying. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (PFPDT).

## Changes

If a new service processes data, we will complete this policy before the service opens, and the update date will change.

## What this page does not cover

This page does not describe how the external sites we link to (Fedlex, FOPH (OFSP), priminfo, cantonal administrations) process data. Their own policies apply. The reference text is the [nLPD on Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/fr).
