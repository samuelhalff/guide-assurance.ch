In 2027, an employee must be insured under the LPP (occupational pensions, 2nd pillar) once their annual salary from one employer exceeds CHF 23,040, and the coordination deduction is CHF 26,880 (Federal Council, press release of 02.10.2026). The minimum interest rate rises from 1.25% to 2% (Federal Council, 04.11.2026).

The employer joins a 2nd-pillar pension institution, most often a collective foundation, through an affiliation contract.

## Who must be insured under the LPP?

Every employee whose annual salary from a single employer exceeds the entry threshold must be insured. Death and disability risks are covered from 1 January after the 17th birthday, and old-age savings start on 1 January after the 24th birthday (art. 7 para. 1 LPP).

## What are the LPP figures for 2027?

| Amount | 2026 | 2027 | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry threshold | CHF 22,680 | **CHF 23,040** | Official (Federal Council, 02.10.2026) |
| Coordination deduction | CHF 26,460 | **CHF 26,880** | Official (Federal Council, 02.10.2026) |
| Minimum interest rate | 1.25% | **2%** | Official (Federal Council, 04.11.2026) |
| Upper limit amount | CHF 90,720 | **CHF 92,160** | Official (art. 5 OPP 2, RO 2026 547) |
| Minimum coordinated salary | CHF 3,780 | **CHF 3,840** | Official (art. 5 OPP 2, RO 2026 547) |

The 2025 and 2026 amounts come from art. 8 para. 1 and 2 LPP. They follow the maximum AVS (old-age and survivors’ insurance) pension, which rises from CHF 2,520 to CHF 2,560 per month in 2027.

## What are the minimum old-age credits?

They are a percentage of the coordinated salary that depends on age (art. 16 LPP):

- ages 25 to 34: 7%;
- ages 35 to 44: 10%;
- ages 45 to 54: 15%;
- ages 55 to 65: 18%.

## What can you compare between pension institutions?

The first criterion is the type of institution: a collective foundation whose risks are reinsured or covered autonomously, or a semi-autonomous foundation. The funding ratio, the interest actually paid on savings beyond the minimum rate and the administration costs per insured person then vary from one institution to another. The plan (LPP minimum or above-minimum benefits), the conversion rate applied to the above-minimum part and the death and disability benefits, with the waiting period before a disability pension, complete the picture.

## What are the common pitfalls?

Four situations cause problems regularly.

1. For employees with several jobs, each salary is examined separately, employer by employer (art. 7 LPP, “a single employer”). A reform is under consultation (motion 22.3389), with no decision so far.
2. With a funding ratio below 100%, the institution may have to take recovery measures.
3. Leaving an institution has consequences for pensioners, depending on the rules of the affiliation contract. Those rules are worth reading before you switch.
4. During sickness or maternity leave, the coordinated salary is maintained at least for the period during which the employer must keep paying the salary (art. 8 para. 3 LPP).

## How do you terminate an affiliation contract?

The affiliation contract sets its own notice periods, often 6 months for the end of a year. It also governs the consequences for pensioners and insured persons, which is why it is worth reading before terminating.

No public data gives contributions foundation by foundation. The FSO (OFS) publishes a pension fund statistic, and the Occupational Pension Supervisory Commission publishes a report on their financial situation.

## What this page does not cover

This page does not compare named foundations and calculates no contribution. It does not deal with the reforms under way. The minimum conversion rate stays at 6.8% at age 65 in 2027 (art. 14 para. 2 LPP).
