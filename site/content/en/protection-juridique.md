Legal protection insurance pays lawyer, court or expert costs in a dispute, in the areas set out in the contract. It is optional in Switzerland. Art. 167 OS (Insurance Supervision Ordinance) provides for free choice of lawyer when proceedings require a representative or in case of a conflict of interest.

## Is legal protection insurance compulsory?

No. Legal protection insurance is a private-law contract governed by the Insurance Contract Act (LCA). There is no regulatory figure for 2027.

## Can you choose your own lawyer?

Yes, in two situations: when court or administrative proceedings require a representative, and in case of a conflict of interest. The contract must then provide for free choice of lawyer. If the insurer refuses the lawyer you named, you may propose three others, and the insurer must accept one of them (art. 167 OS).

## How do two contracts differ?

The first point is the list of areas covered, for example private life, traffic, employment, tenancy or internet-related disputes. Then come the ceiling per dispute, the waiting period before cover starts, the excess you pay and cover abroad. Many contracts also set a minimum amount in dispute below which the insurer does not step in.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. A dispute that has already arisen or is foreseeable when the contract is concluded is not covered, and the waiting period comes on top of that. Insurance taken out once the conflict has broken out comes too late.
2. Family law, inheritance law and construction are often excluded.
3. Similar cover may already exist through a bank card, a trade union or a tenants’ association, which is worth checking before you sign.

There is no public data on legal protection premiums, so contracts are compared on their general terms and conditions, in particular the areas covered, ceilings, exclusions and waiting period.

## When can you cancel legal protection insurance?

The contract can be cancelled for the end of the 3rd year, then for the end of each year, with 3 months’ notice (art. 35a LCA). After a claim, cancellation is possible under the conditions of art. 42 LCA. The right of withdrawal lasts 14 days from the application or acceptance of the contract (art. 2a LCA).

## How is it different from personal liability insurance?

Personal liability insurance (RC privée) pays for damage you cause to others, while legal protection insurance helps you assert your rights or defend yourself. Some situations involve both. For liability insurance, see [Household and personal liability insurance](/en/household-and-liability-insurance/); for online risks, see [Personal cyber insurance](/en/personal-cyber-insurance/).

## What this page does not cover

This page compares no premiums and gives no legal advice on a dispute. It does not detail the other rules of the Insurance Supervision Ordinance that govern legal protection insurance. The reference text is [art. 167 OS on Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2005/735/fr#art_167); your general terms and conditions cover the rest.
