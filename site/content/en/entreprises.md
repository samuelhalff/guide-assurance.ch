A business that employs staff in Switzerland must take out two federal insurances: occupational pensions (LPP, 2nd pillar), for employees earning more than CHF 23,040 a year in 2027 (Federal Council, press release of 02.10.2026), and accident insurance (LAA, Federal Accident Insurance Act). The other lines are optional, unless a canton, a collective labour agreement (CCT) or a professional rule requires them.

## What does each line of cover do?

Six lines come up most often, and two of them are compulsory for every employer.

- [Occupational pensions (LPP)](/en/business/occupational-pensions/) are compulsory for employees whose annual salary with you exceeds CHF 23,040 in 2027. The minimum interest rate rises to 2% (art. 7 LPP).
- [Accident insurance (LAA)](/en/business/accident-insurance/) covers all your workers employed in Switzerland. Some sectors must insure with Suva, and other businesses choose their insurer (art. 1a and 66 LAA).
- [Sickness benefit insurance (IJM)](/en/business/sickness-benefit-insurance/) is not required by federal law. If its benefits are at least equivalent, it replaces your obligation to pay the salary during sickness (art. 324a para. 4 CO).
- [Business and professional liability (RC)](/en/business/professional-liability/) pays for damage caused to third parties. Business liability covers bodily injury and property damage; professional liability also covers purely financial loss caused by wrong advice.
- [Property insurance](/en/business/property-insurance/) protects stock, goods and machinery, and can cover business interruption. In Vaud, business contents must be insured with the ECA (cantonal building insurance institution) against fire and natural hazards.
- [Cyber insurance for small businesses](/en/business/cyber-insurance/) is optional. It pays crisis costs after a cyberattack, business interruption and liability linked to data.

Hiring cross-border workers or people moving to Switzerland? The [HR kit](/en/business/hr-kit/) is a one-page PDF in French and English on their health insurance deadlines.

## What can you compare between contracts?

Offers differ on the same points in every line of cover. The first is what goes beyond the legal minimum, such as the above-minimum part or supplementary cover. Then come the amounts: insured salary, sum insured and benefit period. During waiting periods and up to the excess, the business pays itself. Finally, the exit conditions set the notice periods and what happens to employees or pensioners.

## What are the common pitfalls?

Most come from a legal rule that was missed or a questionnaire filled in too quickly.

1. The activity falls under Suva, yet the business chose a private insurer for the LAA (art. 66 LAA).
2. The sickness benefit insurance is less favourable than the applicable salary scale, so it does not release the employer (art. 324a para. 4 CO).
3. An extended collective agreement requires an insurance that the employment contract does not mention.
4. The proposal questionnaire contains an inaccurate answer, which lets the insurer terminate the contract (art. 6 LCA, Insurance Contract Act), in cyber as in liability.

## How do you terminate a business insurance contract?

Private insurance contracts (supplementary LAA, IJM under the LCA, liability, property, cyber) can be terminated for the end of the 3rd year, then for the end of each year, with 3 months’ notice (art. 35a LCA). The LPP affiliation contract follows its own rules, set in the contract.
