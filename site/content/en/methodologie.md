This is a translation. The [French version](/methodologie/) prevails.

The premiums on Guide Assurance come from the FOPH, the legal rules from Fedlex and the French contribution for cross-border workers from the Urssaf formula. Each figure carries its year and its source; the limits of the tools are set out below.

## Where do the figures come from?

- Health insurance premiums come from the premium tables of the FOPH (OFSP, Federal Office of Public Health), published on priminfo.admin.ch and opendata.swiss. The FOPH approved and published the 2027 premiums on 29.09.2026. For cross-border workers, we use the premium tables for EU residents.
- Legal rules are taken from the consolidated texts on Fedlex, the official compilation of federal law (LAMal, compulsory basic health insurance; OAMal, Health Insurance Ordinance; LCA, Insurance Contract Act; LAA, accident insurance law; OLAA, Accident Insurance Ordinance; LPP, occupational pensions; OPP 3; CO, Swiss Code of Obligations; LCR, Road Traffic Act; OAV, Vehicle Insurance Ordinance; OS, Insurance Supervision Ordinance). Each article we cite links to the text.
- Annual amounts such as pillar 3a ceilings, LPP thresholds and the AVS pension come from press releases of the Federal Council and of the FSIO (OFAS). AVS is old-age and survivors’ insurance, the 1st pillar.
- The French contribution for cross-border workers follows the formula of the public Urssaf simulator (mon-entreprise.urssaf.fr), published as open source code. We read this logic directly and reused its test cases.
- Cantonal rules come from the official websites of the cantons and of the cantonal insurance institutions.

We use no private source for a rule or a figure that is published as established. When a piece of information comes only from a secondary source, the page says so.

## How do we date and check figures?

- Each page carries an update date, and its figures are those known on that date.
- Each figure comes with its year and its source.
- An amount that we calculated, or that is not yet officially published, is marked as such (for example “amount calculated under art. 8 LPP, official publication expected”).
- A rule we have not yet been able to check in an official text is either left out or presented with that reservation.
- We review the pages when premiums or the law change, in particular when premiums are published each autumn and on 1 January of each year.

## Are the lists complete and how are they sorted?

When a tool shows insurers, the list is complete for the situation you entered and sorted by a displayed criterion (for example the annual household premium). There are no badges, recommendations or paid placements. Guide Assurance compares figures and recommends no contract (art. 182a OS).

## What do the tools not take into account?

### Cancellation letter

- It applies the legal deadlines. Your general terms and conditions may provide more favourable terms, and your contract prevails.
- It does not check whether you have unpaid premiums, which block a change of basic insurer.
- It sends nothing. You print, sign and send the letter yourself.

### Cross-border calculator

- The French social security ceiling for 2027 (PASS) is not yet published, so the 2027 contributions are estimates.
- The exchange rate is an assumption that you can change.
- The premium without accident cover assumes that you request it from the insurer, with a certificate from the employer.
- The calculator does not handle a self-employed, unemployed or retired spouse; the page then shows a warning.
- If your spouse is also a cross-border worker, the calculator only compares the cases where you both make the same choice.
- Price is only one factor. Access to care in each country, cost sharing and the fact that the choice cannot be reversed also matter.

### All tools

The calculation runs in your browser and we do not receive your data (see [Privacy](/en/privacy/)).

## How do I report an error?

If you spot an error or an outdated figure, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and name the page. We correct the page and change its update date.

## What this page does not cover

This page does not detail every calculation line by line. The reference premium data is that of the FOPH on [priminfo.admin.ch](https://www.priminfo.admin.ch), and the legal texts are on [Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch).
