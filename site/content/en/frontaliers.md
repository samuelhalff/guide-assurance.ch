If you live in France and start work in Switzerland, you have 3 months from the start of that work to choose between Swiss LAMal (compulsory basic health insurance) and French health insurance (CLEISS). This option right is in principle irrevocable and binds your whole household. If you stay in the French system, you pay a contribution to Urssaf (the French social contributions collection body).

## What is the deadline to choose?

The deadline is **3 months from the start of your work in Switzerland** (CLEISS, the French centre for European and international social security liaison). The Swiss legal basis is art. 3 para. 3 LAMal, which extends the obligation to be insured to certain people living outside Switzerland.

In Geneva, the Health Insurance Service asks new cross-border workers to return a form within 3 months. If you do not reply within this period, you are enrolled in LAMal automatically. On the French side, you declare the choice with the “Choice of health insurance system” form.

## Can you change the choice later?

In principle no, because once exercised, the option right is final. The official texts provide four cases where it can be exercised again:

1. you take up work in Switzerland;
2. you resume work in Switzerland, for example after a period of unemployment;
3. you take up residence in France;
4. you move from employee status to retired status.

Changing employer without a period of unemployment between the two jobs does not count. Neither does a marriage, a divorce, a birth or a death in the family (CLEISS). These four cases appear in the official “Choice of health insurance system” form (January 2024 version) and on the Geneva Health Insurance Service page.

## Who counts as part of your household?

With LAMal, the members of your family insured in Switzerland are with the same insurer as you, so the premium to compare is that of the whole household. On the French side, your dependants are attached to your enrolment without an additional contribution.

If your spouse works in France, your children come primarily under French insurance. If your spouse is also a cross-border worker, each of you has your own option right and can choose differently.

## Do you pay for accident cover twice?

Not if you are an employed cross-border worker working at least **8 hours a week** for the same employer, because the LAA already covers you against accidents (Federal Accident Insurance Act; art. 13 para. 1 OLAA, the accident insurance ordinance). With the LAMal option, you can ask for a premium without accident cover, on presentation of an employer’s certificate (art. 8 para. 1 LAMal). Your spouse and children remain insured with accident cover.

## How much does each option cost?

LAMal premiums for EU residents differ from those for Swiss residents, and priminfo.admin.ch publishes them country by country. For EU residents, the deductible and the co-payment follow special rules (art. 103 para. 6 and 7 OAMal, the Health Insurance Ordinance). In French insurance, Urssaf calculates the contribution on your income, after an allowance.

In an example calculated by Guide Assurance, a household of four people pays between **CHF 6,452 and CHF 26,857 a year** in 2027 LAMal premiums, depending on the insurer chosen.

The [cross-border calculator](/en/cross-border-calculator/) works out the 2027 LAMal premium for your household with each of the 13 insurers offered to residents of France, sorted by household premium. It also estimates the Urssaf contribution for your income. The calculation is done in your browser, and your figures do not reach us.

## What can you compare?

- The household LAMal premium, insurer by insurer, with or without accident cover depending on your situation.
- The Urssaf contribution for your income.
- What the price does not show, such as where you and your family get treated, the cost sharing in each system, the situation of each household member, and the irrevocability of the choice.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. In Geneva, letting the 3 months pass means automatic enrolment in LAMal.
2. With LAMal, the household total is what counts, so comparing per person gives the wrong figure.
3. If you work 8 hours or more a week, accident cover in LAMal is superfluous, because the LAA already covers you.
4. When the spouse works in France, the children come first under the French system, which changes the household calculation.
5. The choice applies going forward, and premiums and incomes change, so the first-year premium alone is a poor basis for deciding.

## Can you change LAMal insurer?

Yes, if you have chosen LAMal, even though the option right itself cannot be cancelled. A change of insurer follows art. 7 LAMal: for a change on 1 January 2027, the letter must be received by 30 November 2026 at the latest. The [Cancellation letter](/en/cancellation-letter/) tool prepares the letter.

## What this page does not cover

This page does not deal with cross-border workers coming from Italy, Germany or Austria, or with special cases (self-employed, unemployed or retired spouse, cancellation of an automatic enrolment). It does not replace the advice of CLEISS or your cantonal service. The reference sources are the [CLEISS FAQ](https://www.cleiss.fr/faq/droit_d_option_des_frontaliers_france-suisse.html), the [FOPH page](https://www.bag.admin.ch/fr/assurance-maladie-travailleurs-frontaliers-en-suisse) and, in Geneva, the [Health Insurance Service](https://www.ge.ch/assurance-maladie-frontaliers-residant-france).
