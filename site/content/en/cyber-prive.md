Depending on the contract, personal cyber insurance covers online shopping fraud, identity theft, cyberbullying and data restoration. It is optional, governed by the Insurance Contract Act (LCA), and sometimes included in a household insurance contract.

## How do two contracts differ?

The first question is whether online shopping fraud is covered, and up to what amount. Contracts also differ in the help they give to restore your situation after identity theft, in legal and psychological help with cyberbullying, and in whether they pay for data restoration. Ceilings are set per claim and per year. Some contracts are part of household insurance, others are taken out separately.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. The cover may overlap with [legal protection insurance](/en/legal-protection-insurance/) or the [household contract](/en/household-and-liability-insurance/) you already have.
2. A claim can be excluded for negligence, for example if you shared your passwords.
3. Ceilings for fraud are sometimes low compared with the amounts you spend online.

## When can you cancel personal cyber insurance?

The contract can be cancelled for the end of the 3rd year, then for the end of each year, with 3 months’ notice (art. 35a LCA). The right of withdrawal lasts 14 days from the application or acceptance (art. 2a LCA).

## What this page does not cover

This page compares no premiums and gives no IT security advice. For cybercrime figures, the reference is the [National Cybersecurity Office](https://www.ncsc.admin.ch). The [business cyber insurance](/en/business/cyber-insurance/) page deals with points specific to companies.
