When you arrive in Switzerland, you have 3 months after taking up residence to take out basic health insurance (art. 3 para. 1 LAMal, compulsory basic health insurance); within that period, it covers you from the day you arrived (art. 5 para. 1 LAMal). Your other insurance depends on your job, your housing and your vehicle.

## How long do you have to take out health insurance?

You must enrol within the **3 months** following the date you take up residence (art. 3 para. 1 LAMal). If you meet this deadline, cover goes back to your date of arrival and costs for that period are paid (art. 5 para. 1 LAMal). If you are late, cover starts only at enrolment, and you pay a premium surcharge if the delay is not excusable (art. 5 para. 2 LAMal). The canton checks enrolment and can enrol you automatically, as Geneva does.

If you stay less than a calendar year, the insurer can charge an adult CHF 250 per 90-day period as deductible and co-payment (art. 103 para. 5 OAMal, the Health Insurance Ordinance).

Insurers are required to accept anyone who applies for basic insurance. Benefits are the same everywhere, while the premium varies by insurer, region, deductible and model (see [Health insurance](/en/health-insurance/) and [2027 premiums](/en/health-insurance/premiums/)).

## Does your employer cover you against accidents?

Yes, if you are an employee. Your employer insures you under the LAA (Federal Accident Insurance Act) from the start of the employment relationship (art. 1a and 3 para. 1 LAA). If you work at least 8 hours a week for them, you are also covered for leisure accidents (art. 13 OLAA, the accident insurance ordinance) and the accident cover in your LAMal premium can be suspended (art. 8 LAMal). The [Accident insurance](/en/accident-insurance/) page has the details.

## Do you need household and liability insurance?

Federal law does not require household insurance or personal liability insurance (RC privée), but landlords often ask for liability cover (ch.ch).

In Vaud, you must insure furniture and contents with the ECA (cantonal building insurance institution) against fire and natural hazards. If you already have a contract with a licensed Swiss insurer, it can run until its expiry date, at most 5 years after your belongings arrive (State of Vaud).

What to look at is described on the [Household and personal liability insurance](/en/household-and-liability-insurance/) page.

## What insurance do you need for an imported vehicle?

Motor vehicle liability insurance is compulsory before any use on the road (art. 63 LCR, the Road Traffic Act), and you need the insurance certificate to register the vehicle. See [Car insurance](/en/car-insurance/).

## Checklist

- [ ] Basic health insurance taken out within 3 months after arrival (art. 3 LAMal).
- [ ] Deductible and model chosen with full knowledge.
- [ ] Accident cover suspended in LAMal on presentation of an employer’s certificate, if you are an employee working 8 hours or more a week.
- [ ] Personal liability insurance, if the landlord requires it.
- [ ] In Vaud, contents insured with the ECA.
- [ ] Vehicle liability insurance taken out before the first trip, certificate handed in for registration.
- [ ] Membership of a pension fund (LPP, occupational pensions) checked, since it sets your 3a limit (see [Pillar 3a](/en/pillar-3a/)).

## What can you compare?

- The LAMal premium for your region, your age, your deductible and your model.
- The alternative model: PRAXIS, TEL_DIG, PHARM or FLEX from 2027.
- For household insurance, the sum insured, theft away from home and tenant’s liability.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. Waiting until the 3rd month to enrol, then missing the deadline because a document is missing.
2. Paying for accident cover twice, in the employer’s LAA and in the LAMal premium.
3. In Vaud, insuring contents against fire twice, with the ECA and with a private insurer.
4. Choosing a high deductible without knowing what care you will need in the first year.

## When can you change basic insurer after arriving?

Once you are insured, you can change basic insurer for 1 January of each year. For 2027, the letter must be received by Monday 30 November 2026 at the latest (art. 7 para. 2 LAMal). The [Cancellation letter](/en/cancellation-letter/) tool calculates the date and prepares the letter.

## What this page does not cover

This page does not cover residence permits, exemptions from the LAMal obligation for certain residence permits, or the amount of the premium surcharge in case of delay.
