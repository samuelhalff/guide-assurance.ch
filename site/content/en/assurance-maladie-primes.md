In 2027, the average adult premium in French-speaking Switzerland ranges from CHF 460.90 a month in Valais to CHF 610.60 in Geneva (FOPH premium tables). Across Switzerland, for all insured persons, the average premium is CHF 412.00 a month, up 5.0% (FOPH, Federal Office of Public Health, published 29.09.2026).

These basic insurance premiums, approved by the FOPH, apply from 1 January to 31 December 2027.

## How much does basic insurance cost in 2027?

According to the FOPH press release of 29.09.2026, the average 2027 premium is **CHF 412.00 a month**, all insured persons combined, compared with CHF 393.30 in 2026 (+5.0%). By age group, the Swiss average is:

- adults: CHF 487.60 a month (+4.9%);
- young adults: CHF 338.80 a month (+4.8%);
- children: CHF 128.10 a month (+4.6%).

Amounts in CHF per month.

The cantonal average is a FOPH estimate that includes all deductibles and all models, so it is lower than the standard premium. Guide Assurance calculated the “Standard” columns from the FOPH premium tables of 22.10.2026: standard model, CHF 300 deductible, with accident cover, all premium regions of the canton, rounded to 10 centimes. No insurer is ranked.

These cantonal averages were read from the FOPH tables. If they differ, the FOPH’s official cantonal publication and the cantons’ press releases prevail.

## How much do premiums vary between insurers in the same canton?

According to Guide Assurance calculations on the FOPH tables, the standard adult premium varies by 24 to 32% between the lowest and the highest within the same canton (CHF 300 deductible, with accident cover). The choice of insurer therefore weighs almost as much as the canton, even though basic benefits are identical at each of them.

## What can you compare between offers?

- The premium for your profile (premium region, age, deductible and model). A cantonal average says nothing about your personal premium.
- The model (PRAXIS, TEL_DIG, PHARM or FLEX from 2027), which gives a reduction allowed by art. 62 para. 1 LAMal (see [Health insurance](/en/health-insurance/)).
- Accident cover, which can be suspended if your employer already covers you (see [Accident insurance](/en/accident-insurance/)).
- The maximum annual cost, which is 12 premiums plus the deductible and the maximum co-payment of CHF 700.

## What are the common pitfalls?

1. A cantonal average mixes all deductibles and all models, so it is not comparable with an offer.
2. A single canton can have several premium regions, and yours depends on where you live.
3. A high deductible lowers the premium but increases what you pay if you fall ill, so the premium alone says little about an offer.
4. The insurer must receive your cancellation by 30 November 2026 at the latest, and a letter posted at the end of November may arrive too late.

## What is the deadline to change insurer for 2027?

To change insurer on 1 January 2027, your cancellation must be received by **Monday 30 November 2026** at the latest (art. 7 para. 2 LAMal). The [Cancellation letter](/en/cancellation-letter/) tool calculates the deadline and prepares the letter in your browser.

For people who live in France and work in Switzerland, LAMal premiums for EU residents are different. The [cross-border calculator](/en/cross-border-calculator/) compares them for your whole household.
