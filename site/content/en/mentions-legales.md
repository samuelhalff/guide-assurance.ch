## Publisher

## What this site does

guide-assurance.ch is an information site about insurance in French-speaking Switzerland. It explains the rules, publishes dated official figures and offers free calculation tools. It compares and informs, recommends no contract and concludes no insurance (art. 182a OS, Insurance Supervision Ordinance).

The site’s ties with insurers and brokers are described on the page [Our ties with insurers and brokers](/en/independence/).

## Scope of the information

The content gives general information. Your contract, your general terms and conditions and the text of the law prevail over it. Each page shows its update date and its sources, and the figures are those known on that date. The [Methodology](/en/methodology/) page describes how we check and date the information.

The tools (cancellation letter, cross-border calculator) give estimates based on the data you enter. The actual amount of a premium or contribution is the one set by the insurer or the competent body.

## External links

The site links to official sources (Fedlex, FOPH (OFSP), priminfo, cantonal administrations) and sometimes to other sites. West Bay SA has no influence over their content.

## Rights

The texts, tables and tools of guide-assurance.ch are protected. The official data reproduced (FOPH premiums, legal texts) remain subject to the terms of their source.
